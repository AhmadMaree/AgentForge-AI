# Evaluation, Safety, and Alignment of Autonomous Agents: Research Summary

## Benchmarking Methodologies and Frameworks
Evaluating AI safety and agent reliability requires rigorous benchmarks designed to test for harmful outputs, truthfulness, bias, and attack robustness [1]. Major evaluation benchmarks include HELM, HarmBench, and TruthfulQA [1]. Additionally, specialized security evaluation benchmark frameworks have been established for AI agents [3]. 

Recent literature catalogs numerous behavioral agent-safety benchmarks and proposes a six-axis taxonomy of benchmark evaluation methodology to characterize how methodological choices influence safety conclusions [2, 4].

## Reliability, Hallucination, and Drift Management
For production environments, AI agent evaluation frameworks incorporate several specialized techniques and components:
* **Ground Truth Datasets:** Used to validate agent performance against known standards [5].
* **LLM-as-a-Judge:** Employed for automated evaluation of agent responses [5].
* **Drift Detection & Hallucination Scoring:** Designed to monitor performance degradation over time and measure hallucination rates within an evaluation flywheel [5].
