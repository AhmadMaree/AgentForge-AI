### Summary of Execution, Control Flow, and Communication

The frameworks analyzed for multi-agent orchestration—LangChain (often associated with LangGraph), Microsoft AutoGen, and CrewAI—each employ distinct philosophies and mechanisms for agent coordination, task delegation, and control flow [3, 5]:

* **LangChain / LangGraph:** Favors explicit loops, message passing [4], and graph-based control flows [5]. Tutorials and multi-agent orchestration deep dives for this ecosystem focus on state management, routing, memory, tool use, and human-in-the-loop patterns [2].
* **CrewAI:** Utilizes declarative task-based flows [4] and role-based crew coordination [5]. It structures multi-agent workflows around distinct roles and coordinated tasks.
* **AutoGen:** Built around conversational multi-agent loops [5] and multi-agent orchestration patterns [2].
